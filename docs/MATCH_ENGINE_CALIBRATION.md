## Known gap: possession

The stronger side has 51–52% of the ball (target 52–62%). In this model the better team's possessions are shorter, because they reach the box and shoot sooner, so its advantage shows in shots and goals more than in time on the ball. Making passing count more pushes possession up but also makes the stronger team win too often (the strength check above). We kept the result targets and accepted the small possession gap.
